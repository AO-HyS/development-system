# ADR 0038: One recoverable visual grill before bounded critique

Status: candidate for local contract 1.22.0, September 15, 2026.

## Context

The installed 1.21.0 contract routed named design work but did not expose an
observable natural-language seam for visual or mixed grills. Its briefs could
mix user constraints, product facts and agent proposals, and its critic guidance
prohibited scoring while the adopted article used a score as supporting evidence.
The absence of a recorded candidate → seed order also made later comparison and
fresh-thread recovery ambiguous.

The accessible public portion of Anshu Chimala's “How to turn your AI into a
world-class designer” was reviewed through technique 6 on September 15, 2026.
Technique 7's body remained subscriber-only. The readable method advocates broad
exploration, external seed strings, ambitious reference-led prompts, a critic in
fresh context, suitable image/motion media and subtractive refinement. It warns
that critic loops need concrete criteria and bounded convergence checks.

## Decision

Contract 1.22.0 and catalog 0.43.0 publish new immutable versions of
`drive-development-flow`, `grill-with-docs`, `design-direction`, `design-quality`
and the existing `visual-reviewer` role.

- Natural visual and mixed requests route to `design-direction` without skill
  names. One `grill-with-docs` interview owns questions and answer recovery.
- Briefs separate confirmed constraints, verified facts and provisional agent
  proposals. Identity is independent from composition, hierarchy and interaction.
- Open-surface exploration records candidates before `concept-seed`, then a
  comparable render and human selection. Exactly three requested alternatives
  produces three materially distinct options with equal task data and finish.
- Visible references carry a named function. Literal user corrections survive;
  rejection revises the hypothesis without inventing approval.
- Independent critique receives screenshots, task/context, confirmed constraints,
  selected direction and reference functions, but not implementer rationale.
  Constraint conformity and compositional quality are separate judgments. A
  grounded score is allowed only as supporting evidence. One or two initial
  passes establish convergence; stalled gaps change the hypothesis.
- Selection, visual review and verified implementation remain separate states.
  Static images do not prove motion, and a phone-sized comparison does not prove
  product responsiveness.

The `visual-grill-route` command is a pure, read-only behavior seam. It classifies
natural requests, deduplicates decision keys, validates candidate/seed/comparison
order and reports capability limitations. It is not a session manager and grants
no implementation or external-write authority.

## Consequences

The method is self-contained after installation and no longer requires the user
to provide the article. Product briefs remain canonical for product choices;
Impeccable remains authoritative for its visual procedures. Synthetic pilot
outputs and independent visual review are observational evidence from one run,
not a universal ranking of methods or models. Published older artifacts remain
unchanged. Installation and rollback verification use isolated HOME only.
