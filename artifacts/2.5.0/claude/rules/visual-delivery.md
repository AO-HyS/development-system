## Visual plans and deliveries

When presenting a substantive plan or closing nontrivial work, use
`visual-explain`: the approved line and movable window, grounded in actual
scope, evidence and remaining limits, without tables or tabs. Ordinary answers,
progress updates and brief confirmations stay text unless explicitly requested
otherwise. Explicit text-only requests win.

Grill keeps its existing HTML questionnaires, contextual questions, drafts and
submission flow. Only the parent publishes in the owning T3 thread, using
`html_preview` before `html_render` on the exact HTML. Existing browser authority
and review budgets apply; without the capability or authorization, provide text
and a durable HTML path with the verification gap stated.

Nontrivial closures still produce the normal `development-system document`
report and link its persistent `htmlPath`; the inline visual summarizes it
without duplicating the whole report. A visual, selected stage or approval
marker grants no authority and proves no installation, adoption or acceptance.
