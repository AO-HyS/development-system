sessions request Sol 6 High at normal speed. Astra XHigh authors definition and implementation
plans; different fresh-context Astra reviewers challenge the plan and resulting
behavior. Fast researchers provide bounded source facts. Luna 6 High at requested priority speed executes exact packets; Sol 6 Medium executes general settled packets; consequential unresolved decisions receive explicitly assigned

Jev evaluates phase-specific obligations before dispatch and after returned
work, through integration, corrections, final review and closure. Typed answers
do not replace technical judgment. The deterministic runtime binds authorization
to current sources, candidate scope, actor, tool input, dependencies and attempt.

does not certify completed behavior, and reported execution does not certify
acceptance. Every linked ticket and criterion stays represented.

spec-ticket-contract.md preserves requirements between definition and execution
threads. Current files and commands belong to the runtime plan. Nonvisual
features use the same evidence and review chain without invented browser gates.
