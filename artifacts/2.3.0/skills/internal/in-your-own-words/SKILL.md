# In your own words

Restate, in your own words and in the user's language:

1. **What the user wants**: the outcome they will judge, not the steps they named.
2. **The problem behind it**: why it matters now, and what goes wrong without it.
3. **How you plan to get there**: the approach in a few lines, what you will reuse,
   and what you will not touch.
4. **What you are unsure about**: only real ambiguities that would change the
   result, each with the assumption you will make if nobody answers.

Keep it short: a reader should confirm or correct it in under a minute.

When the user invoked this skill, stop after the restatement and wait.
When you run it yourself before planning, continue with the work unless an
ambiguity would change the result or cost a one-way decision; then ask that one
question and wait.
