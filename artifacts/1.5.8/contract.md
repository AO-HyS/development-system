# Development System Contract 1.5.8

Version 1.5.8 retains every guarantee and authorization boundary of 1.5.7. Published 1.5.0 through 1.5.7 bytes remain unchanged.

## Prompt-scoped architecture convergence

- Install one versioned architecture reference pack for the five product-convergence prompts. The pack is comparative evidence, not a universal folder template.
- Only an explicit product-convergence prompt opts into architecture inference. It records `current state -> reference evidence -> fit or mismatch -> inferred decision` and presents derivable architecture choices for correction or approval.
- Ordinary Working Backwards keeps asking about complex unresolved decisions. This patch does not make inference the global default.
- Repository facts, product behavior, risk, data, providers, destructive cleanup, and trade-offs that evidence cannot settle remain questions for the operator.

## Bounded, measurable Codex orchestration

- Keep trivial, localized, reversible work on the parent. Delegate only for genuine independence, a required specialty, or risk-specific independent evidence.
- The normal concurrency ceiling is three subagents, with one writer per non-trivial slice. More concurrency requires a task-specific recorded exception and disjoint ownership.
- Reuse stable task names for follow-ups, prefer event-driven waiting over polling, and close every planned lane as `integrated`, `discarded`, `blocked-with-owner`, or `not-started` before completion.
- When multi-agent v2 is unavailable or adds no value, use a clean direct or sequential fallback. A correct zero-agent decision is part of the evidence.
- Evaluate natural work after five non-trivial candidate runs or five calendar days against comparable historical runs. Do not duplicate product work solely for A/B evidence and do not claim causal savings from uncontrolled history.
- Retain the policy only when verified outcomes do not regress and waits, open lanes, or corrections improve without another coordination signal regressing. Otherwise adjust or collect more evidence.
- Surface at most one first action per repository through Check-in. Pilot evaluation and run measurement remain read-only and grant no repository, tracker, release, production, migration, or paid-service authority.

## Topic-based question protocol

- Product and Technical Grills ask one Topic per turn and may batch one to three mutually related decisions inside that Topic.
- Each decision offers two or three exclusive options, places one evidence-based recommendation first, and includes a concrete example with its impact or trade-off.
- Use Codex's native `request_user_input` payload when it is available in the active collaboration mode. Otherwise present an equivalent chat fallback generated from the same source.
- Never ask the user for repository facts or settled context that the agent can inspect. Persist each answer and any changed assumptions before advancing to another Topic.
- Question composition is deterministic and read-only. It grants no approval, lifecycle transition, tracker write, implementation, or delivery authority.

## Natural-language artifact approval

- Accept ordinary natural-language approval in Spanish or English for the one active Working Backwards artifact without requiring an exact phrase. A clear no-change statement plus a request to continue is approval of that artifact.
- Treat any message containing a question, negation, ambiguity, reported approval, multiple formal gates, or requested revision as feedback rather than approval.
- When praise and requested changes coexist, revise first and require approval of the new artifact bytes.
- Natural approval advances exactly one visible checkpoint and never grants implementation, tracker, pull-request, merge, release, production, or paid-service authority.

## Readable diagrams and complete implementation reporting

- Treat Mermaid legibility as behavioral acceptance, not SVG existence. A realistically wide technical diagram must remain readable in expanded and fullscreen states.
- Fit diagrams to the available viewport only while labels remain legible. When fitting would turn a wide diagram into a thumbnail, preserve a readable minimum scale and expose the rest through pan and scroll.
- Keep inline, expanded, fullscreen, fit, reset, zoom, wheel, drag, and pinch behavior coherent in the offline `file://` Reader without network access or a server.
- Final implementation reports must render explicitly requested prompts and handoffs as reviewable content, not links alone.
- Final reports must include a known-issues ledger whose disposition distinguishes fixed, remaining, external-authorization-blocked, and destructive-authorization-blocked work.

## Product convergence workstreams

- Preserve four named workstreams from Technical Grill through Product Contract, Technical Contract, Implementation Map, Linear tickets, rollout, Check-in evidence, and the final Reader: Product and agent architecture, Convex backend, Observability, and Release Train.
- A workstream may conclude that no change is required only when current evidence and acceptance checks support that conclusion; it may not disappear inside a generic repository audit.
- Until native product-specific Linear teams and keys are explicitly authorized and created, every new or updated issue must use the canonical product project and visible product prefix. Reports must not present a bare shared `AOH-n` key as a complete product identifier.

## Bounded attention quality

- Development Steward selects at most one actionable item per primary repository before selecting a second item from any repository, preserving five-product diversity in the bounded Check-in surface.
- Linear Hygiene remains read-only and cannot claim cleanup from an installed auditor. Live create, update, move, close, and delete operations retain their exact external-write and destructive-cleanup authorization boundaries.
